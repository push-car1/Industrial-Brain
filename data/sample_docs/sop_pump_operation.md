# Standard Operating Procedure: Centrifugal Pump P-101

**Document Type:** SOP
**Equipment:** Centrifugal Pump P-101
**Location:** Boiler Feed Water System, Unit 2
**Revision:** 3.0
**Effective Date:** 2025-06-01

## 1. Purpose
This procedure describes the safe start-up, operation, and shutdown of Centrifugal Pump P-101 in the Boiler Feed Water system.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all operators responsible for Boiler Feed Water Pump P-101 operations in Unit 2.

## 3. References
- OISD-116: Fire Protection in Oil Industry
- OISD-118: Safety in Plant Operations
- Factory Act 1948, Section 21: Fencing of Machinery
- OEM Manual: Kirloskar Brothers Centrifugal Pump CP-250

## 4. Pre-Startup Checks

### 4.1 Mechanical Checks
1. Verify pump coupling alignment is within 0.05mm tolerance
2. Check bearing oil level at sight glass midpoint
3. Confirm cooling water flow at 5-8 LPM
4. Verify all guard covers are securely fastened per Section 21 of Factory Act

### 4.2 Instrumentation Checks
1. Suction pressure gauge reading: 2.5-3.0 kg/cm²
2. Discharge pressure gauge operational
3. Temperature sensor on bearing housing: Ambient + 10°C max
4. Flow meter transmitter calibrated

### 4.3 Electrical Checks
1. Motor insulation resistance > 50 MΩ
2. VFD parameters confirmed per settings sheet
3. Emergency stop button functional and tested

## 5. Start-Up Procedure

### 5.1 Initial Steps
1. Obtain PTW (Permit to Work) from shift supervisor
2. Verify all isolation tags are removed
3. Open suction valve fully (counter-clockwise 5 turns)
4. Open vent valve on pump casing until liquid flows steadily
5. Close vent valve
6. Start pump motor from MCC or VFD panel
7. Verify rotation direction (clockwise viewed from motor end)

### 5.2 Post-Start Checks
1. Discharge pressure: 7.5-8.5 kg/cm²
2. Suction pressure: 2.5-3.0 kg/cm²
3. Motor current: < 45 Amps (rated: 50 Amps)
4. Bearing temperature: < 75°C
5. Vibration: < 4.5 mm/s RMS
6. Seal flush flow: 2-3 LPM

## 6. Normal Operation
- Monitor discharge pressure hourly; log in shift register
- Record motor amps every 2 hours
- Check bearing temperature gun reading every 4 hours
- Verify seal leak rate < 5 drops/minute

## 7. Shutdown Procedure
1. Close discharge valve slowly (50% over 30 seconds)
2. Press STOP on MCC/VFD panel
3. Close suction valve fully
4. If maintenance required, isolate and lockout/tagout
5. Release system pressure through drain valve
6. Issue PTW for maintenance if needed

## 8. Emergency Shutdown
1. Press emergency stop immediately
2. Close suction and discharge valves
3. Report to shift supervisor
4. Complete incident report within 1 hour

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Low discharge pressure | Cavitation, worn impeller | Check suction strainer, inform maintenance |
| High vibration | Misalignment, bearing wear | Stop pump, issue work order |
| Motor overcurrent | Deadhead operation, voltage dip | Check discharge valve position |
| Seal leakage > 5 drops/min | Worn mechanical seal | Plan seal replacement within 7 days |

## 10. Safety Precautions
- Ensure area is well-ventilated
- Wear appropriate PPE (safety glasses, hard hat, safety shoes, hearing protection)
- Verify gas detector is operational before entering pump area
- Never operate pump without coupling guard
- Follow all OISD-116 fire prevention guidelines
