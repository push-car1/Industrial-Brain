# Near-Miss & Incident Log (Q1-Q2 2025)

## NM-2025-001: Slip/Trip Hazard in Unit 2
**Date:** 2025-01-10
**Location:** Unit 2, Pump Area
**Description:** Operator slipped on oil spill near Pump P-101. No injury, but risk of fall into rotating equipment.
**Root Cause:** Seal leak on Pump P-101 not cleaned immediately
**Action:** Spill response kit relocated closer to pump area; seal maintenance scheduled

## NM-2025-002: Hot Work Permit Proximity Issue
**Date:** 2025-02-05
**Location:** Unit 2, Pipe Rack Zone B
**Description:** Hot work (grinding) permit issued within 15m of an area where gas testing had not been completed. Discovered during safety walk.
**Root Cause:** Permit issuer did not check adjacent permit status
**Action:** PTW system updated to show active permits in 25m radius; mandatory cross-check added

## NM-2025-003: Confined Space Entry Without Gas Monitor
**Date:** 2025-03-12
**Location:** Tank T-101
**Description:** Maintenance team entered Tank T-101 for inspection. Gas monitor was not worn as it was left charging. Discovered by safety officer during round.
**Root Cause:** No buddy check before entry; equipment accountability failure
**Action:** Mandatory pre-entry checklist with gas monitor verification; second gas monitor placed at entry point

## INC-2025-012: Gas Leak Near-Miss (see full report)
**Date:** 2025-04-18
**Location:** Unit 2, Pump P-102 Area
**Description:** H2S gas leak from flange gasket; fixed gas detector did not alarm due to calibration drift
**Severity:** HIGH
**Root Cause:** Gasket degradation, sensor calibration overdue, inadequate shift handover
**Action:** Full incident report filed; multiple corrective actions implemented

## NM-2025-004: Overhead Crane Load Swing
**Date:** 2025-05-08
**Location:** Maintenance Bay
**Description:** During compressor rotor removal, the overhead crane load swung within 1m of worker. No impact, but risk of crush injury.
**Root Cause:** Load not properly stabilized before lift; tag line not used
**Action:** Refresher training on crane operations; mandatory tag line use for all lifts >500kg

## NM-2025-005: Nitrogen Asphyxiation Risk
**Date:** 2025-06-02
**Location:** Unit 2, Analyzer Shelter A-101
**Description:** Nitrogen purge line to gas analyzer developed a leak in the enclosed analyzer shelter. Shelter O2 monitor alarmed at 19.1%. Area evacuated.
**Root Cause:** Compression fitting on 6mm nylon tubing loosened from vibration
**Action:** Fittings replaced with double-ferrule type; vibration analysis of analyzer shelter conducted

## Pattern Analysis
**Common Themes Emerging:**
1. **Permit coordination** (NM-002, INC-012): Multiple incidents involve gaps in permit-to-work system
2. **Gas detection reliability** (INC-012, NM-005): Inconsistent gas monitoring coverage
3. **Equipment degradation awareness** (NM-001, INC-012): Seal/gasket failures preceded by subtle signs
4. **Communication gaps** (INC-012): Shift changeovers missing critical process data

**Recommended Focus Areas:**
1. Upgrade PTW system to include automatic conflict detection
2. Implement predictive seal/gasket replacement program
3. Enhance shift handover template with chemical composition data
4. Deploy additional O2 monitors in analyzer shelters
