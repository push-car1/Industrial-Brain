# Incident Report: Near-Miss Gas Leak

**Incident ID:** INC-2025-012
**Date:** 2025-04-18
**Time:** 14:30 IST
**Location:** Unit 2, Pump Area (Near Pump P-102)
**Severity:** HIGH (Near-Miss)
**Reported By:** Vikram Singh, Shift Operator

## Description
During routine inspection rounds at 14:30, operator Vikram Singh detected a strong hydrocarbon odor near Pump P-102. The fixed gas detection system (H2S sensor GD-204) did not trigger an alarm. The operator immediately evacuated the area and notified the shift supervisor.

## Immediate Actions Taken
1. Area evacuated within 2 minutes
2. Emergency ventilation system activated at 14:33
3. Gas detector (portable) reading: 35 ppm H2S (alarm threshold: 10 ppm)
4. Pump P-102 isolated and locked out at 14:40
5. All non-essential personnel cleared from Unit 2
6. Incident command established by shift supervisor

## Root Cause Analysis
**Primary Cause:** Minor flange gasket leak on Pump P-102 suction line.
The gasket (PTFE, 2mm) showed signs of degradation consistent with chemical attack. The pump had been handling a batch with slightly elevated H2S content (60 ppm vs normal 20 ppm) for the preceding 3 days.

**Contributing Factors:**
1. Gas sensor GD-204 was found to have calibration drift (last calibration: 45 days ago; recommended interval: 30 days)
2. Pump P-102 seal water flow was lower than specified (1.5 LPM vs required 3 LPM)
3. The shift changeover log from morning to afternoon shift did not mention the elevated H2S in the process stream
4. Permit to Work PTW-2025-089 for maintenance in adjacent area was still active (confined space entry)

## Equipment Involved
- Pump P-102 (Centrifugal Pump, Kirloskar CP-250)
- Gas Detector GD-204 (Fixed H2S Sensor)
- Flange gasket on suction line (DN80, PTFE)

## Corrective Actions
1. **Immediate:** All flange gaskets on Pump P-102 inspected and replaced where degraded
2. **Short-term:** Gas detector GD-204 recalibrated; all fixed gas detectors in Unit 2 calibration schedule updated to 30-day interval
3. **Short-term:** Seal water flow orifice on Pump P-102 cleaned and flow restored to 3 LPM
4. **Medium-term:** Shift changeover template revised to include mandatory process chemical composition handover
5. **Long-term:** Review of gasket material specification for H2S service (consider spiral-wound gasket upgrade)
6. **PTW Review:** All active permits in Zone 2 reviewed; additional gas monitoring requirement added to confined space permits

## Regulatory References
- OISD-118: Clause 5.4 - Gas Detection Systems
- OISD-116: Clause 7.2 - Flange and Pipework Integrity
- Factory Act 1948: Section 36 - Precautions Against Dangerous Fumes
- DGMS Circular 1 of 2024: Gas Monitoring in Confined Spaces

## Lessons Learned
1. Gas detector calibration drift can lead to false sense of safety - implement automated calibration reminders
2. Process composition changes must be communicated across shifts
3. Proximity of active permits to process leaks needs better coordination
4. Seal water flow monitoring could provide early warning of pump seal degradation
